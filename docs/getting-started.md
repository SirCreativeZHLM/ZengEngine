# Getting started

## Your first game in two minutes
1. Open ZengEngine and choose **Blank World**. Name it and pick a folder for it.
2. Click **Cube** in the Objects list, then the **Blocks** tab.
3. Open **Events**, drag **when the game starts** into the workspace.
4. Open **Control**, drag **forever** under it. Open **Motion**, drag **turn left 15 degrees** inside forever.
5. Press the green flag (or **Start**) above the stage on the right. Your cube spins in the stage. Change the 15 to 3 while the game runs and it slows down.

## The screen
- **Objects** (left): everything in your world. **+ Add** makes cubes, spheres, lights, cameras. **⧉** copies, **✕** deletes (Undo brings it back).
- **Blocks** tab: the scripts for the selected object. Each object has its own.
- **Layout** tab: a map of your world seen from above or the side. Drag things to move them.
- **Stage** (top right): your game runs here, like Scratch. Press the green flag to start, the red square to stop. Click the stage and the keyboard and mouse go to the game. **Big window** plays it in its own large window instead.
- **Inspector** (right, under the stage): exact position, size, colour, shininess, physics. Click empty space in Objects to edit the sky, gravity and quality.
- **Files** (left bottom): import 3D models (.glb), pictures and sounds.

## Ideas to learn from
- **Coin Collector**: keys, score, touching, a following camera.
- **Bouncy Balls**: timers, copies, physics and bounciness.

## When something goes wrong
Problems appear at the bottom of the Blocks tab in plain words. Click one to jump to the block.

## Sharing your game
Press **Export**. On macOS you get a `.app`; on Windows and Linux a folder with a runnable game. Everything is inside, nothing to install. Exports are for the computer you are on.

## Quality
Click empty space in the Objects list, then **Graphics**: Fast, Nice, or Best (adds ambient shadows).
